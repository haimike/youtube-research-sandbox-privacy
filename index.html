YouTube Research Sandbox Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026. Covers version 1.1.1.

YouTube Research Sandbox helps you research videos with a privacy-enhanced embedded player and a local notebook. This policy covers the extension, not YouTube or other extensions.

Information processed on your device

The extension checks the current YouTube URL and video ID to recognize watch pages. It reads the video title to associate notes and exports. It processes native playback and mute state to pause/mute the original player while Research Mode is enabled and restore mute state afterward. Inside the research embed, it reads the media time or seeks when you request a bookmark action. It does not query your YouTube account history, authentication credentials, messages, or third-party analytics scorecards.

Local storage and exports

Chrome local extension storage holds the enabled preference and per-video records containing the video ID, title, URL, freeform notes, optional hook/title/thumbnail/test fields, bookmark labels/times, and update timestamps. Notes may include personal information you choose to type. There is no cloud sync or developer-hosted account. You can edit or clear text and remove individual bookmarks. Copying or downloading Markdown is an explicit user action. Exported files and copied text remain wherever you choose to save or paste them. Editing a video's notes in multiple tabs uses the last saved edit.

Network requests and third parties

The extension does not transmit your notes or stored records to the developer and contains no developer analytics, advertising, or tracking service. Playback loads YouTube's privacy-enhanced player at youtube-nocookie.com. YouTube receives requests and browser/network information needed to load the video and interactions needed for playback. YouTube's privacy practices apply to that service; privacy-enhanced does not mean no data collection.

A narrowly scoped Chrome request rule sends an application-identity Referer header on the extension's embed-frame request, using an HTTPS-formatted identifier based on the extension name and ID. No server is contacted at that identifier address. The signed-in parent YouTube page and any other installed extensions may independently collect activity.

Purpose and limited use

Data handled by the extension is used only to provide research playback, note storage, bookmarks, and user-requested exports. The developer does not sell this data, use it for advertising/profiling, transfer it for unrelated purposes, or use it for creditworthiness or lending. The extension's use of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Your choices and retention

You can turn Research Mode off, edit your notes, remove bookmarks, and export your work. Records remain locally until you edit/clear them or uninstall the extension. Uninstalling removes local extension storage; it does not delete files you exported or text you copied elsewhere. Export before uninstalling. There is no developer-side copy from which notes can be recovered.

Privacy boundaries

Pausing and muting does not guarantee that the native page stops telemetry. This extension does not block or delete watch history and cannot guarantee zero history entries, anonymous viewing, or complete recommendation isolation. Third-party sites, extension tools, and the service hosting this policy have their own policies.

Questions and updates

For questions, use the developer contact shown on the extension's Chrome Web Store listing. This policy will be updated if data practices change.
